About Us

LearnX began with the idea that everyone wants to learn something and everyone can teach something. We at LearnX are on a mission to create a timeless and universal learning ecosystem. This mission began when the LeanX team saw the challenges that Learners face. They are learning through outdated methods, in outdated systems. The team came together to utilize technology to connect Knowers (people who know things), with Learners (people who learn things). We wanted to find a way to empower people to learn and teach. The LearnX team has a range of academic experiences. We have each faced problems in our academic careers that could have been solved with the support of others and with technology. Now, we are here to support Knowers and Learners from all around the globe.
